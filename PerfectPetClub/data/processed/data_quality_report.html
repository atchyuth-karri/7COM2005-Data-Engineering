3. Data Cleaning & Transformation Decisions

  1. Survey Validation & PII Elimination:
  2. All 5 survey columns (email, reported_income, nps_recommend, satisfaction_score, online_comparison_freq) were validated.
  3. All 221 respondents matched 1-to-1 with the customer database.
  4. Numeric survey scores were validated to strictly adhere to the [0, 10] scale.
  5. PII Protection: The raw email field was discarded immediately after mapping to customer_id, preventing personal data leakage into the analytical modeling dataset.
  6. CRM Header Typo Correction:
  7. The misspelled column recieved_datatime was renamed in-memory to received_datetime. The raw source CSV was left completely unmodified.
  8. Platform String Normalization:
  9. Platform values stored as Python string-encoded lists (e.g. "['e-mail']") were cleaned into standardized categories ("e-mail", "telephone", "live-chat").
  10. Delivery Status Discrimination:
  11. Sales records were partitioned into completed orders (delivered_date.notna()) and unfulfilled orders (delivered_date.isna()).
  12. Delivery delay in hours was strictly computed for completed deliveries where delivered_date > expected_date.
  13. Memory-Efficient Server Log Ingestion:
  14. The 67,249-row server log CSV (~12 MB) was processed in streaming chunks of 20,000 rows.
  15. Logins were translated immediately to integer customer IDs; IP addresses and raw user-agent strings were dropped to prevent memory overhead and sensitive data exposure.

8. Temporal Leakage Limitations & Dual-Model Evaluation Strategy

Critical Risk: Lack of Survey Timestamps

  1. Unverifiable Collection Point:
  2. PerfectPetClub_survey.csv contains customer responses without a timestamp or date-of-completion attribute.
  3. Although documented as a "WebApp Popup Survey", it cannot be established whether responses occurred early during the subscription, midway through, or as an exit survey immediately prior to or following non-renewal.
  4. Potential for Data Leakage:
  5. If low satisfaction or low NPS scores were recorded at the moment of cancellation, using these features to predict churn constitutes temporal target leakage (predicting an outcome using data generated as a consequence of the outcome).
  6. Conversely, high satisfaction scores might simply reflect active, happy subscribers responding during renewal.

Methodological Solution: Dual-Model Benchmarking

To critically evaluate this phenomenon in Section 4 (Machine Learning), models must be trained and compared under two distinct configurations:

  • Configuration A — Operational Baseline (31 features + target):
  • Uses strictly transactional, operational, CRM, delivery, and web log features.
  • All operational features have verifiable temporal boundaries (100% occurring between start_date and end_date).
  • Configuration B — Multimodal Full Feature Set (35 features + target):
  • Ingests all operational features plus the 4 survey attributes (reported_income, nps_recommend, satisfaction_score, online_comparison_freq).
  • Evaluates feature importance and performance gain (ROC-AUC / F1) against Configuration A to isolate and quantify the survey's incremental contribution and potential leakage footprint.

Neither configuration should be prematurely claimed to be "leakage-free" without empirical verification and ablation analysis.
